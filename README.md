# Azer: The Path of Salvation

**Azer: The Path of Salvation** is a dark fantasy action-adventure RPG for players who enjoy atmospheric combat, monster encounters, and stories about power, corruption, and survival.

In **Azer**, you play as a cursed young man fighting to survive a world of monsters while searching for a cure before the darkness inside him consumes what remains of his humanity.

---

## Why Azer?

Many action RPGs give players power without consequence. **Azer** makes that power personal: the same darkness that helps you survive is also the force threatening to destroy you.

The result is a dark fantasy adventure where every battle matters because the hero is not only fighting monsters — he is fighting himself.

---

## Key Features

- **Fight through a dark fantasy world**  
  Face dangerous enemies in an atmospheric world built around tension, mystery, and survival.

- **Harness the curse within you**  
  Use the protagonist’s inner darkness as a source of strength while confronting the cost of relying on it.

- **Experience a character-driven story**  
  Follow Azer’s journey as he searches for salvation, uncovers the truth behind his curse, and struggles to preserve his humanity.

- **Explore action-focused RPG gameplay**  
  Battle monsters, progress through encounters, and experience a fantasy adventure designed around player agency and combat.

- **Enter a world with a strong visual and narrative identity**  
  The project combines dark fantasy themes, monster conflict, and a personal redemption arc into a focused RPG experience.
